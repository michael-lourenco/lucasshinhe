# Sports Achievement Layout

## Instructions

1. Prioritize the top 4-6 most relevant achievements.
2. Show year + title + competition context.
3. Use a visual pattern that signals credibility (timeline/cards/list).
4. Keep typography compact and easy to scan.
5. Place achievements before or near sponsorship ask.

## Recommended Patterns

- **Highlight grid:** fast scan of key wins
- **Timeline:** evolution and consistency over seasons
- **Proof block:** curated outcomes tied to future potential
